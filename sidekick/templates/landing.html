FAQ

Good to know

Is the Sidekick actually querying real data?

Yes — it connects to SigNoz's MCP server over JSON-RPC, dynamically discovers whatever tools are exposed, and lets Groq's model decide which ones to call. Nothing is hardcoded or scripted.

What happens when the agent fails?

A SigNoz alert detects the failure pattern and fires a webhook. The auto-healer service retries the operation with optimized parameters automatically — no manual intervention required.

What does it cost to run?

Effectively nothing for a demo. Groq's free tier powers the Sidekick's reasoning, and SigNoz runs self-hosted locally via Docker.

Can I verify an answer myself?

Yes — every tool call the Sidekick makes is shown under its answer, and results can include direct SigNoz trace links, so you can check the underlying data yourself.
